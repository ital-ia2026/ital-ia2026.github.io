AI and Disinformation

Date: 18 June 2026

Room: CB31A

Chairs: Gabriella Pasi (Unimib), Walter Quattrociocchi (Sapienza), Matteo Cinelli (Sapienza), Marco Viviani (Unimib)

Description

The AI and Disinformation workshop examines the dynamics of disinformation in the digital ecosystem and the role of artificial intelligence in both its spread and mitigation. It focuses on social media analysis, automated detection of false or manipulated content, and the study of information diffusion processes. The workshop also considers the broader societal and cultural implications of disinformation.
