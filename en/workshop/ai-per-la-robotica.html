AI for Robotics

Date: 18 June 2026

Chairs: Mario Vento (Unisa), Giovanna Castellano (UniBa), Alessia Saggese (Unisa), Lamberto Ballan (UniPd)

Description

The AI for Robotics workshop explores the integration of artificial intelligence with robotic systems, with a focus on the development of autonomous and adaptive systems. Topics include perception, motion planning, learning, and human-robot interaction. Applications span industrial, service, domestic, and social environments.
