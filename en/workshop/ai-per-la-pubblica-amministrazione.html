AI for Public Administration

Date: 18 June 2026

Chairs: Aldo Gangemi (UniBo), Vincenzo Suriani (Sapienza)

Description

The AI for Public Administration workshop focuses on the use of artificial intelligence to innovate public services and improve the efficiency of administrative processes. It addresses the integration of AI into public sector systems, including digital transformation, interoperability, and public data management. Key topics include service automation, decision support, and intelligent tools for policy-making.
